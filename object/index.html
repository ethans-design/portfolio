Technology Tinder

3 Pet Peeves

01
Flipping bacon

Standing at the stove flipping bacon keeps me stuck downstairs when I'd rather be getting ready upstairs.

Idea Something that cooks and flips the bacon on its own, so it's done by the time I come down.

02
A freezing room in the morning

My room is freezing when I wake up because the AC runs all night.

Idea A button that physically presses my AC remote to shut it off around 6 AM, so the room warms up before my alarm.

03
Dirty counters

Wiping down the kitchen counters is a constant, tedious chore.

Idea A little "Zamboni" for the countertop — a small robot that drives across the surface and cleans it on its own.

Motor Buffet

Enclosures

IDC1

IDC2

I would like to make an enclosed dashboard featuring an LED bar as a shift light for my racing simulator, and I would also want it to double as a mini monitor for my computer that can display music info. I will 3D print an enclosure that cleanly holds all the parts with an easy spot to plug it into the PC.

To make all this happen, I will use SimHub to get all the data from my racing games. It works by sending a compiled line of text with all of the information to my ESP32, which will then display it. I can also have the ESP32 drive the LED bar as a colored RPM indicator that blinks when you reach the optimal shifting point.

  • Board ESP32
  • Screen 4″
  • LED bar 54 × 10 mm
  • Enclosure 3D printed
