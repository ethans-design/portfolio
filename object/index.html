process & documentation

A running record of the work I make in Object this semester — ideation, mini projects, and full projects. Each entry documents both the process and the final result through images, video, sketches, and diagrams, with writing that explains each one.

Every piece of media here is paired with a written note covering what (the subject), how (the process), and why (the concept) — media never stands on its own.
